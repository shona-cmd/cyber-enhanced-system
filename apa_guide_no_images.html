APA Style Basics

APA (American Psychological Association) style is widely used for citing sources in academic writing, particularly in the social sciences, technology, and computing fields.

Key Principles:

  • Author-Date Format: Citations include author's last name and year of publication
  • Consistency: Maintain the same format throughout your document
  • Accuracy: Ensure all details are correct and complete
  • Alphabetical Order: Reference lists are arranged alphabetically by author's surname

Why APA at MTAC?

MTAC students use APA style for research papers, theses, and technical documentation. It's the standard for:

  • Computer Science research papers
  • Information Technology projects
  • Engineering documentation
  • Research proposals and reports

In-Text Citations

In-text citations acknowledge sources within your paper. They appear in parentheses at the end of the sentence or integrated into the text.

✓ Correct - Parenthetical Citation:
Cloud computing has revolutionized data storage solutions (Smith, 2023).
✓ Correct - Narrative Citation:
According to Smith (2023), cloud computing has revolutionized data storage solutions.
✗ Incorrect - Missing year:
Cloud computing has revolutionized data storage solutions (Smith).

Multiple Authors:

Two authors: (Johnson & Lee, 2024)
Three to five authors: (Brown, Davis, Wilson, & Chen, 2023)
Six or more authors: (Martinez et al., 2022)

MTAC-Specific Example:

Research at Makerere University has shown significant advances in IoT security (Okello & Nakimuli, 2024).

Reference List

The reference list appears at the end of your paper and provides complete information about each source.

Book Format:

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of work: Capital letter also for subtitle. Location: Publisher.

Journal Article Format:

Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of article. Title of Periodical, volume(issue), pages.

Website Format:

Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). Title of page. Retrieved from http://www.example.com

MTAC-Relevant Examples:

Book:
Musoke, R. (2023). Cybersecurity fundamentals for African institutions. Kampala: East African Publishers.
Journal Article:
Namusoke, J., & Wasswa, P. (2024). IoT security challenges in Ugandan universities. African Journal of Technology, 15(2), 45-62.

Quick Reference Examples

Common citation formats used in MTAC assignments.

📱 Software/App

Format: Developer. (Year). App name (Version) [Mobile application software]. Retrieved from http://

Google. (2024). Google Maps (Version 6.0) [Mobile application software]. Retrieved from http://play.google.com

💻 GitHub Repository

Format: Username. (Year). Repository name [Computer software]. Retrieved from https://github.com/

Okello, J. (2024). MTAC-IoT-Security [Computer software]. Retrieved from https://github.com/jokello/mtac-iot-security

🎓 Thesis/Dissertation

Format: Author. (Year). Title (Doctoral dissertation or Master's thesis). Retrieved from Database name.

Nakato, S. (2023). Machine learning applications in Ugandan agriculture (Master's thesis). Retrieved from Makerere University Digital Repository.

📄 Technical Report

Format: Author. (Year). Title (Report No. xxx). Location: Publisher.

Uganda Communications Commission. (2024). Cybersecurity assessment report (Report No. UCC-2024-15). Kampala: UCC.
